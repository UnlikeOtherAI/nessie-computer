# Nessie Computer

Nessie Computer lets the agents in your Nessie team work on your own computer:
read the folders you share, run the programs you allow and use the apps
installed there. Everything they can use is decided on the computer, by you.

## How it fits together

- **Nessie** (nessie.works) is where your team, agents and projects live.
  There you choose *who* may send work to this computer.
- **Nessie Computer** runs on the computer. There you choose *what* agents may
  use: folders, programs, websites and local apps. Nessie can't change these.
- The computer connects out to Nessie. Nothing on the internet can connect in.

See [error reporting](https://github.com/UnlikeOtherAI/nessie-computer/blob/main/docs/error-reporting.md) for SDK coverage, configuration and verification limits.
